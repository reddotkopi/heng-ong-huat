      知How it works

      The ritual

      1. Write 0 to 9 on ten slips of paper and fold each one twice, so the numbers are hidden.
      2. Choose who you are asking and introduce yourself, aloud or silently.
      3. Pray with the ten slips (0 to 9) cupped in your palms. Bow until one slip falls out.
      4. Unfold the slip to read the digit.
      5. Throw the moon blocks (博杯) to ask if the digit is right.
      6. Fold the slip back into the pool, so the same digit can come again, and repeat for all four digits.
      7. Give thanks before you leave the altar.

      Reading the moon blocks 博杯

        One 圣杯 is enough for most requests. The strict custom asks for three in a row, which takes about 14 throws per digit. Repeated 阴杯 is a sign to stop and ask again another day.

        Who to ask

        Your ancestors, Tua Pek Kong (大伯公), the God of Fortune (财神爷), Datuk Kong (拿督公), or the deity at your own altar. Guan Yin is left out, because many Buddhists feel she should not be asked for lottery numbers. Offerings to Datuk Kong are kept free of pork and alcohol.

        Using motion

        • Bow: hold the phone flat between both palms, screen up, and bow slowly. Each bow is counted; a slip falls after the third, fourth or fifth bow.
        • Throw: hold the phone firmly and give it one quick toss-and-stop flick, as if releasing the blocks. Never let go of your phone.
        • Every motion also has a button, so you can do the whole ritual by touch, and you can switch motion off at any step.
        • Motion needs the page to be opened directly in Safari or Chrome over HTTPS. Inside the Claude viewer the sensors are blocked, so the app uses touch. On iPhone, tap Allow when asked for motion access.

        Is it fair?

        Each slip is chosen with your phone's secure random generator, and each block lands flat side up or round side up with even odds. Because the slip goes back into the pool every time, every number from 0000 to 9999 is equally likely. The ritual decides which number you receive. It cannot make a number more likely to win.
